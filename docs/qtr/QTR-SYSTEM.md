## II · The Substrate and the Postulates

Everything in the QTR cosmos rests on one bottom layer. Of the four nested frameworks that make up the system — each strictly more general than the last — this is the first and deepest: **QTR proper, the Quantum Theory of Relativity**, the substrate framework that answers the single question *what is the universe made of?* The three layers above it (the five-valued logic `ΛL`, the transfinite tower `PIIU`, and the discipline of vacuum navigation) are all built to make the substrate's one hard edge tractable. Understand the substrate and its postulates, and the rest of the system is commentary on how to live at its limits.

### The substrate: what QTR is

QTR is not a patch that reconciles two working theories; it is the deeper layer from which both descend. Quantum mechanics and general relativity are genuinely incompatible, and QTR resolves that incompatibility by refusing to treat either as fundamental. Beneath both lies a substrate whose only furniture is discrete relational events, and both quantum theory and gravity are recovered as *limits* of how those events are arranged and weighted.

> **QTR — Quantum Theory of Relativity.** The substrate framework. It resolves the incompatibility of quantum mechanics and general relativity by finding a deeper layer from which both emerge — a partial order of relational quantum events.

The most important consequence is stated as a principle in its own right:

> **Emergence.** Spacetime, curvature, expansion, and the metric are not fundamental. They are *coarse statistics* of relational quantum events. (Of the classical theories, entropic gravity came closest to this insight.)

Spacetime, fields, and every object in the cosmos are downstream averages over the substrate. Nothing is built *in* spacetime; spacetime is built *out of* relations.

### The primitive: the relational quantum event

QTR has exactly one primitive. Every other object in the theory is a coarse statistic of it.

> **Relational quantum event (RQE).** The single primitive of QTR. A discrete occurrence whose only content is a placement in a partial order and a complex amplitude relative to a reference frame. Spacetime, fields, and every object are coarse statistics of these.

Two features of this definition carry the whole framework. First, the event is *relational*: it has no absolute properties, only a position among other events and a weighting defined against an internal frame. Second, it is *quantum*: its content is a complex amplitude, not a definite classical value. From these two words — "relational" and "quantum" — the four postulates unfold.

### The four postulates (P1–P4)

The substrate is fixed by four postulates. Each isolates one aspect of the relational quantum event: how events are ordered, how they are weighted, what that weighting is relative to, and how the whole structure comes into being.

| Postulate | Name | Statement |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **P1** | Atomicity | History is a locally finite partial order of relational quantum events. |
| **P2** | Amplitude | Every finite sub-history carries a complex amplitude via a spin-foam sum. |
| **P3** | Frames | No amplitude is absolute — all are relative to an internal quantum reference frame. |
| **P4** | Growth | The order is grown one event at a time by a covariant stochastic rule. This is "becoming." |

**P1 · Atomicity** establishes the raw material and its skeleton. History is *atomic* (made of discrete events, not a continuum) and *locally finite* (any bounded stretch contains only finitely many events). The events are arranged in a **partial order** — some events precede others, but not every pair is comparable. Coarse-grained, this partial order becomes the causal structure of spacetime itself:

> **Partial order.** The causal skeleton of the substrate: events are ordered by which precede which, but not all pairs are comparable. Coarse-grained, this order becomes the causal structure of spacetime.

**P2 · Amplitude** supplies the quantum content. Every finite sub-history is assigned a complex number — a **spin-foam amplitude** — computed as a spin-foam sum over the ways that sub-history could be woven together.

> **Spin-foam amplitude.** The complex amplitude (P2) assigned to every finite sub-history via a spin-foam sum. General relativity is recovered as the continuum limit of this sum; higher terms are what f(R) gravity guesses at by hand.

**P3 · Frames** denies any amplitude a God's-eye value. All weighting is defined against an internal **quantum reference frame**; there is no external, absolute amplitude anywhere in the theory. Observation is therefore intrinsically relational — an amplitude is always an amplitude *relative to* some internal frame.

> **Quantum reference frame.** The internal frame relative to which every amplitude is defined (P3) — no amplitude is absolute. The slow reorientation of this frame across a universe's history is what quintessence models as a rolling scalar field.

**P4 · Growth** makes the substrate dynamical. The partial order is not given all at once; it is *grown*, one event at a time, by a covariant stochastic rule. QTR names this process **becoming** — the substrate's answer to why anything happens at all rather than simply being.

> **P4 growth · becoming.** The fourth postulate: the partial order is grown one event at a time by a covariant stochastic rule. This is the substrate's "becoming." Cosmic inflation and Steady State's continuous creation are both surface shadows of P4.

#### Postulates as re-readings of classical cosmology

A recurring habit of QTR is to re-read familiar cosmology as a *surface shadow* of a substrate postulate:

- **`f(R)` gravity** is a hand-guessed stand-in for the higher terms of the P2 spin-foam sum.
- **Entropic gravity** is the classical theory that came nearest to the Emergence principle.

> **Effective layer.** The shallow, field-theoretic description of reality that holds at the OCT-0 surface. All classical physics lives here. It is true but shallow: valid until event density climbs toward the seam, where field language fails.

The effective layer is **true but shallow**. It is not wrong; it is simply an averaged description that holds only while event density stays low. The system is explicit and disciplined about where that description stops being trustworthy — a design principle it calls the **honest boundary**:

## III · ΛL — The Five-Valued Logic

`ΛL` — read *the Logic of the Limit* — is the second layer of the QTR system, the formal reasoning apparatus that sits directly above the substrate of relational quantum events. It is a **five-valued truth algebra**, denoted `𝕍₅`, built for one specific job that classical logic cannot perform: reasoning about a proposition *as it approaches, arrives at, and crosses a limit*. Where ordinary physics hands off to invented physics — at the seam `𝔍` — ordinary logic hands off to `ΛL`. Its five values are not a matter of taste or convenience; the system's central claim is that they **map exactly onto the five physical regimes of QTR**, so that the logic and the cosmology are two readings of one structure.

### Why two values are not enough

Classical logic is bivalent: every proposition is either true (`T`) or false (`F`), and the law of non-contradiction guarantees that asserting both explodes into nonsense. That is adequate in the calm regime, far from any boundary. But the QTR world is built around boundaries — the seam where the effective field description fails, the IR chart-edge that classical cosmology mistook for the Big Bang, the curvature limit `Κ` where QTR's own account of law gives out. At these limits, bivalence breaks down in three characteristic ways that `ΛL` is designed to capture:

- **Approach has a direction.** A proposition nearing a limit is not simply "still true." It matters *which side* it is approached from — from the low-density infrared or the high-density ultraviolet — because the two approaches describe physically different regimes. Bivalence has no vocabulary for "true, but on the way to a limit from below."
- **Contradiction at the seam does not explode.** During a crossing a ship is genuinely *here and there at once*. A bivalent logic must call this false; `ΛL` needs a value at which a contradiction is stable rather than catastrophic.
- **Bivalence is recovered, not assumed.** Beyond a seam the world does become definite again — but this restoration is an *event*, a rule that fires, not a background guarantee. Classical logic cannot represent the moment two-valuedness switches back on.

> **`ΛL` — the Logic of the Limit:** a five-valued truth algebra (`𝕍₅`) for reasoning at, across, and beyond boundaries, where classical bivalence fails. Its five values map exactly onto the five physical regimes of QTR. `ΛL` extends the classical two to five — the *minimal* set needed to track a proposition through its whole life at a limit.

### The five values

The values arrange as a ladder around the seam. `T` and `F` are the familiar classical poles at the two extremes; the three interior values chart the passage between them, with the self-dual seam-value `T•` at the center. This is the canonical display order used throughout the codices — from the calm-true regime, down through the infrared approach, across the seam, up through the ultraviolet approach, to the false pole.

| Value | Name | Physical regime | Meaning |
|-------|------|-----------------|---------|
| `T` | Classical true | Calm regime, far from any limit | Ordinary "true." Also the epistemic status of the Absolute Verdict on `Κ5` — settled, taught, and self-sealing. |
| `T⁻` | Left-approach · IR | Infrared (low-density) approach | The truth-value of approaching a limit from the low-density side. The state of a ship's early approach to the seam. |
| `T•` | Seam-truth · `𝔍` | At the seam itself | The self-dual value *at* the seam: `¬(T•) = T•`. The logical form of being in two places at once during a crossing. |

> **`T` — classical true.** The ordinary "true" of the calm regime, far from any limit.

> **`T⁻` — left-approach · IR.** Approaching a limit from the infrared (low-density) side. This is the `ΛL` reading of inflation and MOND, and the state of a ship's early, gentle run at the seam.

> **`T•` — seam-truth.** The self-dual value at the seam: `¬(T•) = T•`. A contradiction does not explode into falsity — it produces more seam-truth. This is the logical form of being in two places at once during a crossing.

> **`T⁺` — right-approach · UV.** Approaching from the ultraviolet (high-density) side — the status of theories reaching toward the substrate *from above*, such as entropic gravity and the multiverse, and of the Madman's Theorem on `Κ5`.

> **`F` — classical false.** Ordinary "false" — the value of models QTR supersedes outright (Steady State, tired light), and the value toward which "this region obeys our laws" drifts as it nears the curvature limit `Κ`.

### Ordering and the two approaches

The ladder is not a linear "more true → less true" scale. It is a **regime ladder** organized around the seam, symmetric about the central value `T•`. The two interior approach-values, `T⁻` and `T⁺`, are mirror images: both describe *nearing* a limit, but from opposite densities. `T⁻` is the approach from the **infrared**, the low-density side; `T⁺` is the approach from the **ultraviolet**, the high-density side. A proposition tracked through a crossing therefore does not slide monotonically from `T` to `F`. It leaves the calm `T` regime, enters an approach value (`T⁻` or `T⁺`), reaches `T•` at the seam, and — on the far side — is resolved by the Resurrection rule back to a definite `T` or `F`. In the `Κ`-approach the drift is written explicitly as `T → T• → F`: near the curvature limit, "this region obeys our laws" degrades from true, through seam-truth, to false, and unlike the seam's clean springback this refusal has no return.

### Operators and inference rules

`ΛL`'s distinctive behavior is concentrated in a few rules stated directly in the source:

- **Seam paraconsistency (negation at `T•`).** The one explicit operator identity is the self-duality of negation at the seam: `¬(T•) = T•`. In a paraconsistent logic a contradiction does not trigger explosion (*ex falso quodlibet*); here it does the opposite — asserting both sides of a proposition at the seam yields *more* seam-truth, not falsity. This is what lets a mid-crossing ship consistently hold the value "here **and** there."
- **Resurrection.** Beyond a seam, classical bivalence is *restored*: a superposition at `T•` collapses to a definite `T` or `F`. When a ship re-condenses at its destination, the Resurrection rule is firing — the ship is definitely **there**. At cosmic scale this same rule is what the Big Bounce enacts, the universe re-condensing to a definite state on the far side of `𝔍` rather than reaching a singularity.
- **Seam incompleteness.** `ΛL` proves its own incompleteness *as an internal theorem*. Prior formal systems (Peano arithmetic, ZFC) are incomplete in a way they cannot see from inside; `ΛL` sees its own seam — described as closing a loop open since Gödel, 1931. The same reflexive structure — a system that can name its own boundary but not cross it — recurs in the top of the tower `OCT-Ω` (the theory's own Gödel sentence) and in the unmeasurability of `Κ`.

The system also names **four core inference rules** as the operational spine of `ΛL`: **Anti-Transfer**, **Both-Sides Agreement**, **Seam Paraconsistency**, and **Resurrection**. These four are not arbitrary — they are the logical counterparts of the four physical postulates, as set out below.

The difference between the doors is therefore not what they are made of but what they change: the seam/Idrenes Bridge changes your **depth** (and returns you); the Penrose bridge changes your **region** at the same depth (one-way); and `Κ` changes the **law** (contested). ## VI · Navigation and Vessels

Navigation is the fourth and outermost layer of the QTR operational stack — the layer that answers *what a pilot actually does*. Where `QTR` describes the substrate, `ΛL` the logic of its limits, and `PIIU` the transfinite hierarchy of state-depths within one universe, `NAV` is the operational discipline of moving a ship through that structure. Its governing insight is that the quantum vacuum is neither empty nor a fluid but a **structured medium of correlations, modes, thresholds, and horizons** — a sea with weather and terrain. A vessel does not accelerate its way faster; it *dives deeper* into the vacuum's state-structure, where the route between two points becomes topologically short. Speed is depth.

> **The cardinal rule of QTR travel.** A vessel never locally exceeds `c`. The Special-Relativistic relation `dτ = dT·√(1 − v²/c²)` holds everywhere, locally, without exception. Apparent superluminal velocity (`v_app = d_ij / τ_ij`, which *may* exceed `c`) is purely a **Field-topology effect** — the Field route is short, not the light-speed high. Superluminal navigation is therefore never travel *in* time: light bounds ordinary spacetime, while the Field is the navigable substrate laid over it.

### The two axes of navigation

A ship steers on two independent axes, and the whole of seamanship is configuring boundary conditions along them:

- **Longitudinal axis** — the *route within a regime*, threaded through local geometry. This is ordinary in-layer sailing: finding and riding favorable currents without changing the vacuum's state-depth.
- **Vertical axis** — the *state-depth of the vacuum*, i.e. which regime the ship inhabits. The vertical axis is the **operational path to the seam**; descending it is what leads to a crossing.

A third kind of motion — **the seam-crossing between state-depths** — is not one of these two navigational axes but the *event* that occurs when the vertical axis is driven all the way to its threshold. In the wider cartography this resolves into three distinct doors, each on its own axis (see [Cartography]): the **depth seam** `𝔍` (the Idrenes Bridge, a dive between OCT rungs from which you return), **adjacency** (a Penrose bridge, a sideways move to a remote region of the same universe, one-way per throat), and the **constitution frontier** `Κ` (the internal limit past which QTR cannot say what a region becomes — approached, never confirmed crossed). Navigation proper operates on the first; the routine seam is not the curvature frontier.

> A note on orientation. The pilot's operational chart **dives downward** — the calm surface on top, the seam `𝔍` at the bottom. The Atlas map draws the same state-depth axis *inverted* (substrate floor below, seam and `Κ` above), so a dive on the pilot's chart is a climb on the Atlas. They are one axis drawn two ways.

## VIII · Time and Reckoning

Time in the QTR cosmos is not a fundamental backdrop against which events happen. It is a consequence of two of the system's deepest postulates, and it behaves accordingly: it is **relational** and it is **grown**. This layer — the *Temporal Layer* of the Operational Codex, published as "The Reckoning of Time," Rev 1.0 by the Pelagian Assembly — sets out how the cosmos tells "when," why the further one travels the less "meanwhile" means, and how a civilization builds a workable substitute for the universal clock reality refuses to provide.

The whole layer condenses to a single load-bearing sentence, repeated across the sources as its one-line principle:

> A body keeps its own time faithfully — and the world's time is the thing that slips.

Because the physics rests on a substrate of pure relation in which nothing has absolute properties, time inherits that groundlessness. The result is the layer's central theme: connection across difference is never given; the further two people travel apart, the more the very fact of a shared "meanwhile" dissolves. Every kind of travel is, at bottom, a story about coming unmoored from the people who stayed — and the work of being **re-tuned** to them.

### The two postulates time inherits

Time in QTR is governed by two postulates carried over from the foundational physics:

- **`P3` — Relational.** No frame is absolute. "Now" for one observer is not "now" for another; it is *negotiated, not shared*. Two people do not disagree about a common clock — they have **no common clock**, and must build a correspondence between their times. This is the origin of the **no-universal-clock** principle that governs the entire layer.
- **`P4` — Grown (Becoming).** Events are not laid out in advance along a pre-existing timeline; the substrate *lays down new events* at a rate. That rate is not uniform. It runs **faster deeper down the tower**: a deeper `OCT` level has a shorter effective Planck length, so more events are laid per unit of proper time.

> **The no-universal-clock principle.** The cosmos cannot offer a single clock against which all others are compared. Time runs at different rates at different depths, and there is no absolute frame to reconcile them. Every temporal fact in QTR is therefore either *relational within a shared lattice* or *fundamentally indeterminate across frames* — never absolute.

### The Three Clocks — what a person can feel

Ordinary physics has one clock. QTR has three, and a single character can feel all three at once. The first never lies; the second runs faster the deeper one goes; the third is not a rate at all but a relationship — and it is where the strangeness lives.

| Clock | Name | What it is | Felt as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 · Always True** | **Proper time** | The felt duration of a single observer — heartbeat, breath, the length of a thought. It exists, it works, and it is *never* perceived to change. Nobody ever feels themselves slow down. | **Nothing unusual.** Your own time is the one thing you can always trust — which is exactly the trap, because it means everything *else* is what appears to move. |
| **02 · The Deep Clock** | **Becoming-time (`P4`)** | The rate at which the substrate lays down new events. Runs *faster down the tower*: a deeper `OCT` level has a shorter effective Planck length, so more events are laid per unit of proper time. The clock with no real-world analogue. | **Density, not speed.** A diver cannot feel "faster" — she feels the world happening *harder*, more per breath than a breath should hold. The shear between her rate and the world's is the vertigo divers call *running under*. |
| **03 · Not a Rate** | **Relational offset (`P3`)** | Because no frame is absolute, "now" for one observer is not "now" for another — it is *negotiated, not shared*. Two people have no common clock and must build a correspondence. | **Desynchronisation.** Not "you aged, I didn't," but "our times have come unmoored, and there may be no fact about which is right — only the relationship we rebuild." |

The disciplined rule beneath all three: a character can **never** feel time "speed up" as an absolute. She can only ever feel density, surplus, and shear — the felt correlates of relative rates. That restraint is what keeps the fiction faithful to the `P3` no-universal-clock canon.

### The Three Sensations of Time — felt-language

The codex's abstractions have a matching body of felt-language, written to render the three distinct ways a body registers time coming unmoored. They are three separate wounds in three separate registers — pointedly *not* the tired "you aged and I didn't."

#### I · Dive-vertigo ("running under")

What a pilot feels descending the tower, where becoming runs faster than her body is tuned to. The first mark passes and nothing changes — pulse, cabin, readouts all hold — because her body is the one thing still keeping its own time; everything *else* accelerates. By the third mark she feels the world beginning to *happen harder*: not faster (there is no clock in her skull to register thinned seconds) but denser, more occurring per breath than a breath should hold. She breathes once while a thousand more events are laid down in the substrate she falls through, and feels the surplus as pressure behind the eyes. Deeper still comes the vertigo proper — not dizziness but the sense of being a slow thing inside a fast world, her own becoming lagging the world's, the two rates sliding past each other like trains at different speeds while her stomach registers the shear.

> **Running under.** Old divers' slang for the desync of a deep dive — *being under the world's rate*, the world becoming faster than the body. The felt signature of depth travel. "You are under the rate. The world is ahead of you and getting further ahead and there is nothing to hold."

The emotional signature is an asymmetry: **going under is frightening; coming up is lonely.** Descent is vertigo; ascent is grief.

#### II · The seam-gap

What a crosser carries — the un-narratable hole a crossing leaves in a life. Asked what the crossing was like, the honest answer is *I don't know* — not forgetting (forgetting leaves a smudge) but something cleaner. He remembers the approach (readouts sliding off true, field-language going soft) and the far side (the snap of re-condensing arrival, "the way you come up out of anesthesia already in the middle of a sentence"). Between them there is nothing to remember, because he was *here and there at once* for a duration that has no length, and a mind cannot lay that down as memory.

> **The seam-gap.** The un-narratable interval of a seam-crossing — held at `ΛL` value **`T•`**, it cannot be laid down as memory because bivalence was suspended there. A life gains one seam-gap per crossing. It sits in a life as "a join with no width, where the story of himself skips a stitch and picks up clean on the other side."

The character arc this unlocks: frequent crossers are people whose autobiography has holes at every crossing. One who crosses forty times carries forty dropped stitches — "the parts of his life he was most alive for, and the only parts he will never be able to tell." Those who cross for a living become "mostly seam," and go quiet in a particular way. It is not damage; so much of them happened in places that cannot be spoken.

#### III · Surfacing-thinness

The specific loneliness of return — kept distinct from dive-vertigo because it is a different wound. During descent she was too busy being afraid to be sad; home is where the problem starts. Everything is too slow — the light lazy, the station's hum spaced out and unhurried — and she, still running fast from the deep, sits inside it "like a hummingbird in a room of sleeping people, quick and wrong and alone with her quickness." It passes, as her rate and the world's find each other again over the next few marks. But in the thin cold minutes before the world catches her back up, there is **no shared *now*** to stand in with the people she loves — just her, ahead of them, waiting to be slow enough to be with them again. Vertigo is the shear *during* descent; thinness is the relational loneliness *after* return.

A full travel-and-return sequence runs: **dive-vertigo** (going down) → the mission → optionally a **seam-gap** if she crosses while deep → **surfacing-thinness** (coming home). Three distinct wounds, three distinct registers — all guaranteed by the same physics: a body keeps its own time faithfully, and the world's time is what slips.

### The Two Regimes — how to relate travellers at different depths

This is the rule the story runs on: *which axis a character travels decides which kind of time they suffer.* The spatial structure that sorts the cosmos does double duty as two theories of time. Depth-travel gives comprehensible, high-stakes dilation; seam- and adjacency-travel give eerie, permanent desynchronisation.

| | **Regime A · Determinate** | **Regime B · Indeterminate** |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Within one universe — across depth | Across the seam — or to a causally-remote region |
| **Governs** | The depth axis · the dive | The seam `𝔍` · the adjacency axis |
| **Comparable?** | Yes — shared lattice | No — no shared frame |
| **Offset** | Determinate & measurable | Fundamentally indeterminate |
| **Reunion** | "You aged more than me" — a fact | No truth of "who waited" — only trust |
| **Drama** | Twin-paradox loss, legible | Desynchronisation, permanent |

**Regime A (Determinate).** A diver and a surface-dweller in the *same* universe share enough substrate correlation that their becoming-rates *can* be compared. Depth-dilation holds like a clean twin paradox — but **inverted**: the diver, immersed in faster becoming, comes home **older**, not younger. The one who went deep aged *more*. The offset is a number, and you can put a figure on what was lost.

**Regime B (Indeterminate).** Cross `𝔍`, or take a Penrose bridge to a causally-remote region, and the two charts share no frame. There is **no fact** about how much time passed "meanwhile" back home. Reunion is not measurement but renegotiation, and the ambiguity is permanent — the relationship must be rebuilt, because the arithmetic that would settle it does not exist.

> **The Hybrid Rule.** Depth-axis travel is *determinate*: same tower, same lattice, correspondence exists, the diver ages more. Seam- and adjacency-travel are *indeterminate*: different frames, no correspondence, no fact of the matter. The structure built to sort *space* now sorts *time* — and which wound a character carries is decided by which door they went through.

### The Beacon Network — how a civilization agrees on "when"

The cosmos cannot offer a universal clock, but a civilization can build the next best thing: a network of the steadiest natural timekeepers there are. **Beacon cores — pulsars —** keep relational time so cleanly that fleets anchor `ΛL` phase to them. Promoted to an institution, they become the backbone of shared time: **reference beacons.**

- **What it gives — a diegetic coordinate time.** Ships log every dive and crossing against a shared pulse. Characters can say "she surfaced four beacon-cycles ahead of me," so a reader can track offsets without any universal clock ever existing.
- **Its fragility — coverage has an edge.** The network only spans regions with *enough mutual information* to carry the phase. Dive deep enough, or travel far enough, and the beacons **fail** — and there is genuinely no shared time. **Regime B begins exactly where coverage ends.** (In the network's own diagram: an inner zone of synced ships in "shared time · determinate," phase drift growing outward, and beyond the rings a ship "beyond coverage · no shared now.")
- **Its danger — whoever holds the beacons holds "when."** In a civilization that cannot otherwise agree on time, controlling the reference network is real power: the power to define the official "now" and to declare whose account of a reunion is the true one.

### Units of Reckoning — the vocabulary of "when"

The working horology characters actually use — precise near the beacons, vaguer at the edge, and frankly mythic beyond it.

| Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Beacon-cycle** | The base unit: one full phase-period of the reference network. The shared "second" of the civilization. *Only meaningful inside coverage.* |
| **Proper-watch** | A duration measured by a ship's own clock, independent of any beacon — what the crew actually lived. Compared to beacon-cycles, it reveals the dive's dilation. |
| **Running under** | Pilot's slang for the desync of a deep dive — being *under the world's rate*, the world becoming faster than the body. The felt signature of depth travel. |
| **The gap** | The un-narratable interval of a seam-crossing — held at `ΛL` value **`T•`**, it cannot be laid down as memory. A life gains one **seam-gap** per crossing. |
| **Offset** | The measured difference between two parties' clocks on reunion. Determinate within a universe; *undefined across the seam*, where it becomes a matter of trust, not arithmetic. |
| **Dark time** | Any duration spent beyond beacon coverage, for which no shared reckoning exists. A ship that returns from dark time returns to a "when" no one can agree on. |

### The Two Timelines — determinate vs indeterminate parting

The second half of this layer is an interactive model titled *Two Timelines · Determinate vs Indeterminate* — "two timelines, two kinds of parting." It is **not** a chronology of historical epochs; it is a side-by-side model of one stayer and one traveller, showing how the choice of axis parts them by a *measurable* amount or an *undefined* one. One character travels; one stays; the **stayer's clock is the reference.**

The model runs a journey from the surface (`OCT-0`) downward (or outward past the seam), with the stayer logging the journey length in beacon-cycles and the traveller's line either packing denser or fraying into a fan:

| | **Depth · Determinate** | **Seam · Indeterminate** |
|---|---|---|
| **Traveller role** | Diver | Crosser |
| **Depth anchor** | `OCT-0 · surface`, deepening with the dive | `at the seam 𝔍`, then "beyond `𝔍` · adrift" |
| **Stayer elapsed** | The journey length in beacon-cycles (baseline of elapsed time; the reference clock) | Same reference clock — she waits at the surface |
| **Traveller elapsed** | *More* than the stayer — immersed in faster becoming, she lives more per breath; her line's ticks pack denser the deeper she goes | **— undefined —** — her clock and her sister's no longer share a frame |
| **Offset on reunion** | A settled, measurable count of beacon-cycles (`Δ` grows as a number) | **`?`** — no fact of the matter; the frames share no clock. Trust, not arithmetic |
| **Reunion verdict** | "Offset defined" — she comes home measurably older | "Offset undefined" — her future frays into a fan of possible durations, none privileged |
| **The crossing itself** | (none) | Leaves a **gap** she cannot narrate — held at `T•`, it never became memory |

In the determinate case the traveller's line stays parallel and rejoins the stayer's, with a reunion connector labelled by the offset `Δ` in cycles; the deeper the dive, the denser her ticks and the larger the number. This is the twin paradox *inverted* — the one who travelled aged **more**, not less. Legible grief, with a figure attached.

In the indeterminate case her line reaches the seam `𝔍`, passes through the un-narratable **gap** (held at `T•`), and then frays into a fan of possible timelines — no single tick count, the reunion smeared across a band of endpoints and the offset marked only `Δ ?`. Her family cannot even be told how long to have grieved.

> **The three story-wounds, by axis.**
> - **Depth · Regime A — the diver's return.** She dives; her sister stays. Same universe, so the offset is real: she comes home older by a measured count of beacon-cycles. A legible grief — the twin paradox, inverted.
> - **Seam · Regime B — the crosser's homecoming.** He crosses `𝔍` and returns. There is no fact about how long he was gone; the frames share no clock. The reunion is rebuilt on trust, because the arithmetic to settle it does not exist.
> - **Dark time · The Edge.** A ship goes past coverage into dark time and returns to a "when" no one can agree on. Whoever controls the beacon network decides the official account — so a private loss becomes a political fact, and time itself is contested ground.

### The narrative-present anchor

These sources fix **no calendar date and no universal "year zero"** — consistent with the `P3` no-universal-clock principle, they cannot. The reckoning is anchored *relationally* rather than absolutely: the operative reference frame is `OCT-0 · surface`, the stayer's frame — the surface-dweller "who never left," whose clock the whole model treats as the reference against which every traveller's offset is read. "Now," in the QTR system, is not a point on a master timeline but the stayer's own proper time, from which all other times must be renegotiated. The layer's closing formulation states the whole reckoning in one line:

> Proper · Becoming · Offset — determinate within, indeterminate across. The world's time is the thing that slips.

### Six ways a universe may end

Every universe samples one ultimate fate from an **observationally-weighted prior**. ΛCDM makes heat death most likely; Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology is the escape. The weights are the model's sampling prior (`FATES` + `sample_fate`):

### The aeon lifecycle — Penrose Conformal Cyclic Cosmology

Proper time of each aeon is infinite; its conformal description is finite. The remote future of one aeon is conformally matched to the Big Bang of the next — `T(U_k) → U_{(k+1) mod P}` (with `P` prime), a linked succession over a prime-length cycle, **not** a bounce or crunch. > Hot beginning (aeon origin) → Galaxies & stars (stelliferous) → Black holes (degenerate) → Extreme dilution (massless future) → **Conformal crossover (aeonic edge · model boundary)** → Next hot beginning (aeon `k+1`)

**MODEL ASSUMPTION:** if it occurs, this is a possible continuation of *this* universe's cosmological lifecycle — not routine movement among parallel universes, and emphatically **not** the routine depth-seam `𝔍` that ordinary ships cross daily via the Idrenes Bridge.

The **infinity of infinities** (`INFINITY_TAXONOMY`):

| Kind of infinity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Infinite future within each aeon | proper time of each aeon is infinite; its conformal description finite |

### The hybrid stochastic model

Deep time is not one inevitable future but an **ecology of possible histories** — probability distributions over millions to billions of years. Four interacting methods drive it:

### How deep time maps onto the QTR cartography

The framework is deliberately a lexicon over the cartography — three correspondences carry the whole mapping:

**`Φ` tiers ↔ doors.** Each Field rung is a specific codex capability, so a civilization's `Φ` names which door it can open. `Φ₀` is the *effective layer / OCT-0 surface*; `Φ₁` is *ER = EPR signalling*; `Φ₂` is *Casimir-corridor sailing*; `Φ₃` opens the **depth seam `𝔍`** (the Idrenes Bridge, the routine dive between OCT rungs); `Φ₄` kindles **seam-wells** (a black hole lit into a held-open gate); `Φ₅` is **cosmological Field integration & universe-scale cognitive networking**; `Φ₆` is **universal self-reference & possible trans-universal awareness** (any becoming-time / postulate-P4 effect staying conditional). Adjacency — a sideways Penrose bridge to a remote region of the same universe — is the third door, distinct from both.

**The aeonic edge · `Κ` relabel.** The deep-time model's **conformal crossover** between aeons corresponds to the cartography's **aeonic edge** — a speculative, cosmological-scale transition (**MODEL ASSUMPTION**), read through the constitution frontier `Κ`, *not* the routine depth-seam `𝔍`. It is a possible continuation of *this* universe's cosmological lifecycle, not confirmed movement to another universe. The Tragic-Truth verdict at that edge — imagining a pattern (never the substance) translated beyond the system's descriptive limit — is one interpretation; reading it as a "different parallel chain" is a **POSSIBLE EXTENSION**, not established physics, and is how the model *optionally* realizes the codex's "infinity of infinities" as recurrence.

**Vessels ↔ eras.** Because a ship's class equals the maximum vacuum depth it can reach, each era's `Φ` tier corresponds to the fleet it can field: Casimir Sailers (`Class I`) sail the surface corridors of the Diaspora (`Φ₂`); the dive down the OCT rungs (`Φ₃`) is the standard of the galactic and Oceanic ages, opened by the Idrenes Bridge; Penrose Frontier Ships (`Class II–III`) hold the threshold `𝔍` and the deep-field crossings that the Eonic age makes routine; the Idrenes Composite (`Class II–ω`) reaches down to the causal substrate. The OCT ladder (OCT-0 surface → OCT-1 deep-vacuum coherence → OCT-2 cosmological reach → OCT-ω transfinite) is the same ladder the vessel classes (`I → II → III → ω`) and the Field tiers climb — one structure named three ways. Civilization is the process; the Field is the constant.

## X · The Languages — Sūchel and the Family

The QTR world is not monolingual. A civilization that can very nearly cross its entire universe does not speak one tongue — it speaks a **family**, all descended from a single proto-language, **Old Pelagic** (`Pelgar`), and all shaped by the same physics but worn differently by each community that carries it. The languages are built the way real languages are built: a proto-tongue, ordered and regular sound changes, and grammars that *grammaticalize what their speakers cannot afford to be vague about*. The governing principle, stated across the codex, is that a language marks what its world makes unignorable — Quechua marks evidence, Guugu Yimithirr marks compass direction, and the tongues of the crossable universe mark the truth-value of the limit, the anchor of time, the direction of depth, and the place where narration fails.

The family's guiding slogan: **you cannot speak without conjugating the truth.**

### The Language Codex

The Language Codex (page 09) presents **Sūchel** `[ˈsuː.tʃel]`, "the Crossing-Speech," the working vernacular of the fleets, alongside its liturgical ancestor. It is built on the "Peterson Method" premise that a language is its speakers. (The Peterson Method here is a *linguistic* construction method only — building the tongue from speakers, history, sound change, and grammaticalization; it is not QTR's mathematics or physics foundation, nor a derivation of the cosmology.)

#### The two tongues, one family

> **Old Pelagic · `Pelgar`** — *the ancestor, liturgical.* The tongue of the pre-crossing civilization, the surface era, when the deep (`*pelag-`, "the open deep") was still only ocean and sky. It is rigid SOV, fully inflected, phonologically conservative. When the first crossings shattered the old cosmology, the **Pelagian Assembly** froze this language as its liturgical and doctrinal register — catechism, verdicts, and law are pronounced in Old Pelagic to this day. To speak doctrine in the old tongue is a claim: *the truth has not moved since before the seam.*

> **Sūchel · `*suw-kel` "crossing-speech"** — *the daughter, fleet vernacular.* Two dozen generations of fleet life eroded Old Pelagic into Sūchel, the working language of divers, crossers, and beacon-keepers. Same bones, worn smooth by **seven regular sound changes** — but where the Assembly's tongue stayed still, Sūchel grew **new grammar** for the new world: truth-moods for the five values, time-anchors for the three reckonings (beacon, proper, dark), depth-words for the tower, and a particle for the gap no verb can enter. The pilot argot ("running under," "dark time") is its slang register.

Headline figures: proto-language **Old Pelagic**; **7 regular** sound changes; **5** veridical moods (tied to `ΛL`); **3** temporal anchors; a lexicon of **77 entries** in the codex's searchable dictionary.

#### Diachrony — seven sound changes

Every Sūchel word descends from an Old Pelagic form by these seven ordered, regular changes. Crucially, the pre-existing "canon names" of the setting fall out as regular outcomes.

| # | Change | Rule | Example |
|---|--------|------|---------|
| SC-1 | Palatalization | `*k, *g` → `ch, j` before front vowels e, i | `*kel` "speech" → `chel`; `*gel-` "stitch, join" → `jel` "the seam `𝔍`" |
| SC-2 | Medial syncope | Unstressed short vowel lost between consonants | `*ideren-es` → `Idrenes`; `*pelag-ar` → `Pelgar` |
| SC-3 | Glide loss | `*w` → ∅ before C with **compensatory lengthening**; `*w` → v word-initially | `*suw-kel` → `Sūchel`; `*sow-orn` → `Sōrn`; `*wer-` → `ver` "truth" |
| SC-4 | H-loss | `*h` → ∅, lengthening a preceding vowel | `*mahin` → `mān` "hand, crew-hand" |
| SC-5 | Monophthongization | `*au` → `ō` | `*haw-ol-` → `hōl` "to surface" |
| SC-6 | Lenition | Intervocalic `*p` → `v` | `*kap-a` → `kava` → `kav` "tolerance; the limit `Κ`" |
| SC-7 | Apocope | Final short vowel lost after a heavy syllable | `*tem-a` → `tem` "pattern"; `*kava` → `kav` |

Three canon derivations are highlighted:
- **`Sōrn` — the drive.** `*sow-orn` "thread-instrument" (o-grade of root `*suw-/*sow-` "to thread through, cross" + instrumental `*-orn`) → SC-3 glide loss with lengthening. The engine is literally "the threader"; its zero-grade sister `*suw-kel` gives the language's own name, so drive and tongue are cognates.
- **`Idrenes` — the bridge.** `*id-` "open" + `*ren-` "path" + `*-es` "place of" = `*ideren-es` "place of the opened path" → SC-2. "Idrenes Bridge" is thus a pleonasm, like "Sahara Desert," which fleet speakers find quietly funny.
- **`jel` — the seam `𝔍`.** `*gel-` "to stitch, to join" → SC-1 palatalization → `jel` `/dʒel/`, which is why the scholars' symbol is `𝔍`. Derivatives: `jelmar` "seam-pearl," `jelvos` "seam-scar," `jelsīl` "seam-crosser." The universe's deepest object wears the humblest word: a tailor's.

#### Core grammar — the bones

Sūchel is **verb-final (SOV), agglutinative, postpositional**, inherited straight from Old Pelagic. Nouns are simple; the verb carries the world.

**Nouns.** Plural `-i` (`sīl` "crosser" → `sīli`). Case clitics: accusative `-(e)n`, genitive `-en`, dative `-ol`, locative `-eth`. Sūchel splits possession: alienable things take genitive `-en`, but kin, one's ship, and one's *gaps* take the **entangled suffix `-mai`** — possession as shared amplitude, not ownership. `kel-en` is "my words," but `nav-mai` is "my-entangled ship"; to use `-en` of your own ship is to announce you plan to sell her. Pronouns: `en` 1SG, `ish` 2SG, `an` 3SG (+ `-i` plural, `-mai` entangled). `enmai` is the "crew-we, the entangled we," distinct from `eni`, the mere plural — a captain who says `eni` of her own crew is understood to be resigning.

**Verbs.** The template is:

> **STEM – (ASPECT) – MOOD = ANCHOR**

Aspect: perfective `-t-`, imperfective unmarked. Mood: one of the five veridical moods — **obligatory**. Anchor: one of the three temporal clitics — **obligatory on finite verbs**. Negation is the particle `vo` before the verb. Example: `an sū-t-eshe=zu` (3SG cross-PFV-`T•`=DARK) — "She crossed," where the verb alone confesses: seam-true, in time no beacon reached.

#### The four systems — where the cosmology lives in the verb

These four grammatical systems are the language's reason to exist.

**System 01 · `ΛL` in the verb — the five veridical moods.** Every finite verb conjugates for the `ΛL` status of its proposition; there is no mood-less verb.

| `ΛL` | Suffix | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| `T` | `-a` | classically true; settled |
| `T⁻` | `-im` | true-as-approached; provisional from below (IR) |
| `T⁺` | `-ur` | held-from-above; theoretical, unaccredited (UV) |
| `T•` | `-eshe` | seam-true; self-dual, both-and |
| `F` | `vo` + `-a` | false (negated plain) |

**System 02 · three reckonings in the clitic — the temporal anchors.** Every finite verb must declare which time it happened in; the wrong anchor is treated as perjury by fleet courts. (These three anchors are the *reckoning units* of Ch. VIII — beacon, proper, and dark time — not the metaphysical **Three Clocks** of proper / becoming / offset; only “proper” is shared between the two triads.)

| Clitic | From | Anchor |
|--------|------|--------|
| `=ka` | `*kad-` "pulse" | **Beacon time** — shared, network-verifiable |
| `=mi` | `*mei-` "self" | **Proper time** — the ship's own clock |
| `=zu` | `*dzu-` "adrift" | **Dark time** — beyond coverage; unanchorable |

**System 03 · the tower in motion — depth directionals.** Like Guugu Yimithirr's compass, Sūchel motion is **tower-absolute**. Verbs of movement are ungrammatical without a depth satellite: `nuv` "down-tower, deeper" (`*nub-`) or `hau` "surfaceward" (`*haw-`). Bare "go" is baby-talk. From `nav nuv ran-eshe=mi` ("The ship runs deeper") comes the lexicalized pilot slang **`nuvran`**, "running under" — the dive-vertigo itself.

**System 04 · the gap in syntax — the particle `ne`.** It is *syntactically forbidden* to place a verb inside the narration of a crossing: the interval held at `T•` never became memory, and the grammar refuses to pretend otherwise. The particle `ne` (`*nex-` "absence") stands where the verb cannot, and in fleet orthography it has its own mark (a short vertical stroke). Example: `en jel-eth tel-t-eshe=ka. ne. en hōl-t-eshe=zu.` — "I reached the seam. — . I surfaced, in dark time." The life between the two sentences has no sentence.

#### The Showpiece — `Κ5` as conjugation

One proposition, three moods, the whole `Κ5` controversy compressed into morphology:

- **The Absolute Verdict** (Assembly doctrine): `tem kav-eth vo dur-a=ka.` — "No pattern endures at the limit." Plain `T`, beacon-anchored; doctrine claims network-verifiable knowledge of the one event no beacon has witnessed.
- **The Madman's Theorem** (heresy): `tem kav-eth dur-ur=zu.` — "A pattern endures at the limit." `T⁺`, dark-anchored; the grammar itself brands the claim unaccredited, so to *say* the theorem is to conjugate yourself a crank.
- **The Tragic Truth** (what actually happens): `tem kav-eth dur-eshe=zu.` — "A pattern endures — seam-true — in dark time." The only honest conjugation; because `T•` is self-dual, the sentence and its negation have the same value. The epistemic trap is not in the debate — it is in the verb.

#### Representative texts

The codex glosses three interlinear texts: **a pilot's log after a crossing** (three sentences whose morphology alone reconstructs the flight plan — crossing conjugated seam-true, departure beacon-anchored `=ka`, return dark `=zu`, with the `ne`-mark between); **a catechism with one heretical answer** (the pupil's heresy is *two morphemes long* — `-eshe` for `-a`, `=zu` for `=ka`); and **a `ne`-liturgy for the unreturned** (mourning structured as gap-marks, the last verb conjugated `T⁻` — "approaching-true," and the dead still possessed as kin via `-mai`).

### Sound (Phonology)

Page 12 codifies pronunciation and rhythm. The aesthetic target is oceanic and worn — long vowels like held breath, soft affricates from old hard stops, clusters sanded down by ship-life. Romanization is one-symbol-one-sound; `ū ō ē ā ī` mark length; `ch j sh` are `/tʃ dʒ ʃ/`.

#### Consonant inventory (16 + marginal /h/)

| **Stop** | p b | t d | | k g |
| **Affricate** | | | ch `/tʃ/` · j `/dʒ/` | |

Old Pelagic additionally had `*w` and `*h`; both died in Sūchel (SC-3, SC-4), leaving long vowels as their tombstones. In Sūchel, `/h/` survives only word-initially (`hōl`, `hau`, `hep`); elsewhere old `*h` has become vowel length.

#### Vowel inventory (5 + length)

| | Front | Central | Back |
|---|-------|---------|------|
| **High** | i · ī | | u · ū |
| **Mid** | e · ē | | o · ō |
| **Low** | | a · ā | |

**Length ≠ quality** — a long vowel is the same vowel *held* (a held breath), never a change in colour. Pronunciation anchors for an English mouth: `a` as in father (short, never "cat"); `e` as in bed (never "ay"-glided); `i` as in machine (short); `o` as in story (pure, no glide); `u` as in rude (short). `r` is tapped `/ɾ/` between vowels and a brief trill `/r/` at word-start, never the English glide; `s` is always voiceless; `g` always hard; `k` lightly aspirated at most.

**Syllable:** (C)(r/l)V(C); no clusters of three.

#### Stress — four rules, applied in order

The first rule that applies wins:

1. **Compounds stress their first member** (on that member's own stressed syllable): `NUV-ran`, `JEL-mar`, `SŌRN-mai`, `E-she-ver`, `ZU-kad`.
2. **Finite verbs: the mood seizes the stress** — stress the syllable containing the veridical suffix's first vowel, so the truth-claim carries the beat; anchor clitics (`=ka =mi =zu`) are weightless and never stressed: `ve-RA-ka`, `ta-NA-mi`, `hōl-TE-she-zu`, `i-DE-she-mi`.
3. **A long vowel seizes the stress** in any other word: `SŪ-chel`, `HŌ-lu`, `MĀN`, `ĀN`.
4. **Otherwise: stress the penult of the stem** — and case suffixes never shift it once set: `I-dre-nes`, `PEL-gar`, `VU-rel` → `VU-re-len` (gen.), `ZU-kad` → `ZU-ka-deth` (loc.).

The defining discovery: **the mood carries the beat** — in Sūchel you can *hear* the truth-claim. The corollary is that, because commands assert nothing, **imperatives are beatless**: `-u` adds no beat, so orders stay flat and stem-stressed (`TA-nu`, `JE-du`, `I-du`).

**Voice direction by register:** Assembly / `T=ka` — measured, resonant, unhurried (liturgy). Bridge imperatives — flat and fast, nearly toneless (`jel id-u` is spoken quietly; you do not shout at the seam). `=mi` reports — intimate, close, inward. `T•` / `=zu` lines — thinned, quieter, breathier, "partly elsewhere." The gap (`ne`, line 38) — a full voiced syllable, then held as silence.

### Script (Writing system)

Page 11 documents **Navcher**, the fleet script — from `nav` "ship" + `cher` "to carve, write" (`*ker-` → SC-1), i.e. "ship-carve," the angular stencil hand of the fleets. Old Pelagic wrote a flowing **tide-script**, boustrophedon (lines alternating direction "as the tide runs"); the fleets linearized it left-to-right for terminals and stencils, demoting the old vowel diacritics to small inline letters. The working system: **17 consonant letters, 5 small vowels** with the held-breath bar, plus operator sigils.

#### Three design rules

1. **Featural.** Letterforms encode phonetics: *head position* = place (labials head-left, alveolars head-center, velars head-right); a *foot-stroke* = voicing; a *slash* = frication. Learn six letters and you can predict the rest.
2. **Stencil-safe.** Every stroke is straight; nothing fully encloses; the script cuts clean from a plate. **The only curve in the entire system is the `=zu` anchor** — the unanchored mark is the one sign that refuses to close.
3. **Two hands.** The *careful hand* spells every suffix phonetically (liturgy, law, log-of-record); the *bridge hand* compresses mood and anchor into sigils (instrument panels, hull marks, haste). Same letters, two registers.

#### The letters

Consonants are grouped by featural logic: **stops** (closed head, voiced adds the foot) p / b / t / d / k / g; **affricates & fricatives** (the slash family) ch / j / s / z / sh / v; **nasals & liquids** m / n / r / l; plus **`he`, the threshold letter** — since `/h/` survives only word-initially, its letter is drawn as *a stem with a gap in it*, the letter that is mostly absence.

Vowels sit half-height between the consonants (the fossil of their diacritic past): `a` the wedge, `e` the level, `i` the tick, `o` the diamond, `u` the cup. Length is the **held-breath bar** drawn above — `a` vs `ā` is "one stroke of patience."

#### The operator sigils

Because every finite verb must carry mood and anchor, bridge hand compresses them into sigils. The five moods are variations on one square:

- `T` (`-a`) — the plain square, settled
- `T⁻` (`-im`) — tick left, IR approach
- `T⁺` (`-ur`) — tick right, UV approach
- `T•` (`-eshe`) — **the filled square, the seam-mark**
- `F` (`vo`) — crossed out

The three anchors: `=ka` the beacon-pulse, `=mi` the chest-stroke (the self's own clock), and `=zu` **the open hook — the script's only curve, unclosed because unanchored**. Additional marks: `ne` the **gap-stroke** (full-height, wide-set — spoken it is `ne`, but written it is *not spelled*: the stroke stands alone as a held silence, or a black panel), and `vu` the rising pair (the question mark).

A sample bridge-hand rendering of litany line 40, `hōl-t-eshe=zu. enmai ve-a=mi.` ("Surfaced — seam-true, in the dark. We are — by our own clock."), makes the whole "Resurrection" legible in four sigils: the filled square (`T•`) then the open hook (`=zu`), then the plain square (`T`) and the chest-stroke (`=mi`).

The phrasebook appendix coins **14 additions** for the master lexicon, each with a proper Old Pelagic source: `jed` "to go" (`*ged-` → SC-1), `tan` "to hold" (`*tan-`), `id` "to open" (`*id-`), `men` "to wait" (`*men-`), `ret` "again" (`*ret-`), `ān` "breath" (`*anh-` → SC-4), `kru` "blood" (`*kru-`), `gal` "the Still, becalmed vacuum / Dead-Sea" (`*gal-`), `lesh` "alignment, fair phase, luck" (`*les-`), `maiel` "kinsman" (`-mai` + agent `-el`), `om` "all" (`*om-`), `vu` question particle (`*wu` → SC-3), `-u` imperative (`*-u` hortative), and `ō` vocative (`*ō` interjection).

### The Sister Languages

Page 13 maps the whole family. The great isogloss — the **palatal shibboleth** — is what each tongue did to old `*k` and `*g` before front vowels; ask a stranger to name the seam and their mouth files their birth certificate.

| Proto → tongue | "the seam" `*gel-` | "speech" `*kel` | "crossing-speech" `*suw-kel` | "Truth-station" `*wer-stan` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old Pelagic / Lorkel | `gel` | `kel` | `Suwkel` | `Werstan` |
| Sūchel · the fleets | `jel` | `chel` | `Sūchel` | `Verstan` |
| Rudgar · Mars | `ghel` | `khel` | `Suwkhel` | `Werstan` |
| Sel · Maren | `zel` | `sel` | `Sūsel` | `Ērstan` |
| Beltsel · Belgar | `dzel` | `tsel` | `Suvtsel` | `Verstan` |

Three worlds keep a proto-era city named "Truth-station": `Werstan` on Mars, `Ērstan` on Maren, `Verstan` on Belgar — the same name through three mouths, used by customs officers as a voice-test. A Wolori scholar tells himself by saying the *unshifted* `gel` — the order is audible in one syllable.

The family tree: **Old Pelagic (`Pelgar`)** — "the tongue before the seam — no moods, no anchors, no `ne`" — branches into **Sūchel** (the fleets, lingua franca; says `jel`; 7 changes), **Rudgar** (Mars, old ground; says `ghel`; keeps `*w *h`), **Sel** (Maren, ocean moon; says `zel`; drowns `*w`), and **Beltsel** (Belgar, the capital; says `dzel`; "cannot hold its breath"). Beneath them Old Pelagic survives twice: frozen in Assembly liturgy, and *working* in the Wolori's `Lorkel`. Above all speech sits **Notation, the zero-tongue** — no moods, no anchors, no speakers.

#### Rudgar — Mars, the conservative sister

`Rudgar` (`*rud-gar`, "red ground" — world and tongue share a name) is the **conservative sister**: it never lost `*w` or `*h`, the two sounds every other daughter killed, so Martian ears are the closest living ears to Old Pelagic. Its sound changes:

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| R-1 | `*w, *h` retained everywhere | `*wer-stan` → `Werstan` |
| R-2 | `*k, *g` → `kh, gh` initially & intervocalically | `*kel` → `khel`; `*gel` → `ghel` |
| R-3 | All final short vowels lost | `*kap-a` → `khap` |
| R-4 | `*au` → `ā` | `*hau-` → `hā-` "upper" |
| R-5 | Unstressed medial `*e, *o` → a (dry leveling) | `*pelag-ar` → `Pelaghar` |

The fleets' `Sōrn` is Mars's `Soworn` (glide kept), `Sūchel` is `Suwkhel`, the seam is `ghel` — the tombstone is still alive on Mars. Twenty place-names are charted (`Rudgar`, `Khades` "Beacon-stead," `Hekmor` "High-rock," `Akdol` "Ice-pit," `Ghelren` "Seam-path," `Pelaghar` "the Deephold," `Werstan` "Truth-station," and others — sampled here).

#### Sel — Maren, the liquid sister

`Sel` is spoken on **Maren** (`*mar-en`, "of the pearls"), an ocean moon. Where Mars preserved, Maren dissolved: the family's most fluid sister, long, open, tide-paced. Sound changes:

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| S-1 | `*k, *g` → `s, z` before front vowels | `*kel` → `sel`; `*gel` → `zel` |
| S-2 | `*w` → ∅ everywhere, with lengthening | `*wer-stan` → `Ērstan` |
| S-3 | Intervocalic `*p, *t, *k` → b, d, g | `*kap-a` → `kaba` |
| S-4 | `*h` → ∅ with lengthening | `*hek-mar` → `Ēkmar` |
| S-5 | Final short `*i, *u` lowered → e, o; finals kept | `*kad-u` → `kado` |

`Sūsel` is Maren's name for the crossing-speech; `*sow-orn` comes out `Sōrn`, identical to the fleet form — the two tongues agree by accident of sound law on the drive's own name, which Maren folk take as a good omen for engines. Twenty place-names are given (`Maren`, `Zelmor` "Seam-rock," `Ērstan` "Truth-station," `Nās` "Ship-place," `Ānes` "Breath-place," `Zeles` "Seam-place," etc. — sampled).

#### Beltsel — Belgar, the eroded sister

`Beltsel` (`*bel-kel`, "hearth-speech") is the prestige tongue of **Belgar** (`*bel-gar`, "Hearth-ground"), the administrative capital — the family's **fast sister**, sanded down by court and bureaucracy. Sound changes:

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| B-1 | `*k, *g` → `ts, dz` before front vowels | `*kel` → `tsel`; `*gel` → `dzel` |
| B-2 | Unstressed non-final short vowels lost | `*pelag-ar` → `Plagar` |
| B-3 | Three-consonant clusters simplify (middle drops) | `*kad-stan` → `Kadtan` |
| B-4 | `*w` → v everywhere, no lengthening | `*wer-stan` → `Verstan` |
| B-5 | `*h` → ∅, no lengthening | `*hek-` → `ek-` |
| B-6 | **All long vowels shorten** | `*nāw-ren` → `Navren` |

The defining trait (B-6) is the one the other sisters mock: **Beltsel lost vowel length entirely — the capital cannot hold its breath.** Say `Sūchel` to a Belgar clerk and they file it as `Suvtsel`; the length you hold is, to them, a spelling error. Twenty place-names are charted (`Belgar`, `Dzelren` "Seam-way," `Kadtan` "Beacon-stand," `Verstan` "Truth-station," `Nexbel` "Gap-hearth, where the `ne`-liturgies are sung," etc. — sampled).

#### The Wolori — language of the Irrationals

The order of science that follows Logos — the *Ricercatori del Limite*, called the **Irrationals** — speaks the ancestor, but Old Pelagic survives in **two hands**, and the difference is the whole politics of truth:

- **Custodian 01 · The Assembly — Liturgical Pelgar, frozen.** Performed, not lived; the grammatical claim that truth has not moved since before the seam; the tongue of the consensus (Doxa), whose creed is *«tutto è connesso con tutto»*. Sacred precisely because it is blind to the new world: it cannot conjugate a crossing, so in it no crossing can contradict doctrine.
- **Custodian 02 · The Wolori — `Lorkel`, working Old Pelagic.** `*lor-kel`, "ratio-speech": the Irrationals' living technical register, extended by coinage the way Neo-Latin coined *oxygenium*. Same corpus, opposite use — the Assembly embalmed the ancestor, the Wolori keep it *employed*.
- **Custodian 00 · Notation — the zero-tongue.** Above `Lorkel` sits mathematics itself: no moods, no anchors, **no native speakers** — the only medium in which `T•` can be written without being sworn. Members take notation-names (`√2, π, φ, √−1, z`), pronounced differently on every world but written identically — the one perfect identity in a crossable universe.

The design thesis: **the moodless tongue is the scientific one.** In Sūchel you cannot state a hypothesis, because every finite verb must swear a mood, so a conjecture is grammatical perjury. Old Pelagic predates the seam — no veridical moods, no temporal anchors, no `ne` — so it is the only tongue in which a proposition can be *entertained without being asserted*: the hypothesis-space language. Hence the mirrored joke: the Assembly keeps Old Pelagic because it cannot describe the seam; the Wolori keep it for the same reason — one uses the blindness to protect doctrine from evidence, the other to protect inquiry from doctrine. The endonym **Wolori** (`*wo-lor-i`, "the un-ratioed") is Doxa's sneer ("Irrationals") worn as a badge — an order devoted to the Ratio, named for what escapes ratio, because their founding discovery is that *the truth is incommensurable with the consensus grammar* (Bone's uniform bears «√2» for exactly this reason).

**Neo-Pelagic** (`Lorkel`) grows by compounding proto-roots with no sound changes applied. Eight standard terms:

| Lorkel | Formation | Meaning | Sūchel reflex |
|--------|-----------|---------|---------------|
| `gel` | `*gel-` | the seam `𝔍` (unshifted) | `jel` |
| `kaplor` | `*kap-lor` | "holding-rule" — the curvature limit `Κ` as law | `kav` |
| `pewlor` | `*pew-lor` | "five-rule" — the logic `ΛL` | `pevlor` |
| `gelwer` | `*gel-wer` | "seam-truth" — the value `T•` | `eshe-ver` |
| `turom` | `*tur-om` | "all-tower" — the OCT entire | `Turmai` |
| `nexlor` | `*nex-lor` | "gap-law" — the crossing interval admits no predicate | `ne` |
| `suwath` | `*suw-ath` | "a crossing" — the event, stated flat, unmooded | `sūath` |
| `wolor` | `*wo-lor` | "the un-ratioed" — an incommensurable; a truth outside the five moods | *no reflex* |

An open (not-yet-canon) proposal places the Wolori motherhouse on Mars at `Pelaghar` ("the Deephold"), making Rudgar their cradle vernacular — fitting, since the conservative sister reads Old Pelagic with the least accent.

### Nubhel — the Deep-Speech

Page 14 presents **Nubhel** `[ˈnub.hel]` (`*nub-kel`, "deep-speech"), Sūchel's sister and rival — the language of the **Nūbi**, the vertical fleet, who hold that the Speed Law's real teaching is *depth, not crossing*. Same proto-language, same physics, but each fleet **grammaticalized the regime it lives in**: the crossers live in the indeterminate regime (gaps, `T•`, unanchored time); the divers live in the determinate one (dilation, arithmetic, coming home older by a number).

#### The two fleets

- **The Sīli · crossers · Sūchel** — the horizontal fleet. Seam-oriented; prestige is the crossing and the `T•` mood, their wound is the gap; their time is punctuated by `ne`-holes no one can narrate. They call the divers `nuvi` ("the aged"). Sneer: *"divers go fast to stay in one universe."*
- **The Nūbi · divers · Nubhel** — the vertical fleet. Depth-oriented; prestige is *class* (how deep a hull can hold), their wound is the offset (they age past everyone they love, by a measurable count). They call the crossers `Sūl` ("the gapped," "hollow-lived"). Sneer: *"crossing is a shortcut; depth is the truth."*

#### Diachrony — seven changes, with one ordered before another

The key ordering: **D-3 is ordered *before* D-1** — original `*h` dies first, then new h is born from `*k`, so every medial h in Nubhel is *secondary*, a diagnostic for philologists. And where the capital shortened its vowels, the divers lengthened theirs: **Nubhel is the tongue that holds its breath.**

| # | Change | Rule | Example |
|---|--------|------|---------|
| D-1 | Deep palatalization | `*k` → `h`, `*g` → `y` before front vowels (**after D-3**) | `*kel` → `hel`; `*gel` → `yel`; `*ged-` → `yed` |
| D-2 | Fortition | Word-initial `*w` → `b` | `*wer-` → `ber`; `*wur-` → `bur` |
| D-3 | Breath-loss | All `*h` → ∅, and non-initial `*w` → ∅, with compensatory lengthening | `*haw-ol-` → `ōl`; `*suw-kel` → `Sūhel` |
| D-4 | Pressure-rounding | Stressed `*a` → `o` | `*kad` → `kod`; `*tan-` → `ton`; `*ran-` → `ron` |
| D-5 | Held-breath drift | Vowels lengthen in open stressed syllables | `*nub-i` → `Nūbi` |
| D-6 | Sinking | `*au` → `ū` | `*hau` → `ū` "up" |
| D-7 | Apocope | Final short vowel lost after a heavy syllable | `*tem-a` → `tem` |

The minimal pair: `*suw-kel` gives **`Sūchel`** in one mouth and **`Sūhel`** in the other — the whole cognate gap in one phoneme. But `*sow-orn` → `Sōrn` in *both*: everyone agrees on the engine. Two mergers become jokes: D-2 merges `*wel` "nothing" with `*bel` "hearth" (`bel` means both — *"the hearth is nothing to us"*), and D-4 merges `*mar` "pearl" into `*mor` "rock" (`mor` covers both — the divers *"cannot tell a pearl from a stone,"* which they take as a compliment).

#### The grammar of depth

The verb template is unchanged (STEM–(ASP)–MOOD=ANCHOR), but every slot drifted for the determinate regime.

**System 01 · three moods — and a hole.** Nubhel kept three veridical moods: plain **`-ó`** (witnessed, settled), approach **`-im`** (nearing a limit — the divers' home mood), and hearsay **`-ur`** (old `T⁺` "held-from-above" drifted to "held on another's word"). But `*-eshe` left **no reflex**: Nubhel cannot conjugate the seam-truth. Hence **the code-switch rule** — a diver narrating a crossing must *borrow Sūchel* for the `T•` clause; mid-sentence, the rival's language appears exactly where the seam does.

**System 02 · the debt anchor & the signature.** `=ka` (beacon) and `=mi` (proper) are shared inheritance, but `=zu` has no Nubhel cognate (divers rarely leave coverage). In its place: **`=nu`** (from `*nub`), the **deep-time anchor** — "stated in dive-time, conversion owed." Formal register appends the debt as a numeral, the **offset signature**: `en ōl-t-ó=nu, +mek-dok` ("I surfaced — in dive-time; forty owed"). Divers sign letters this way — *Mora, +212* — a life's dilation worn as a name-suffix.

**System 03 · five degrees of depth.** Where Sūchel has two directionals, Nubhel grades five (motion verbs are ungrammatical without one):

| Word | From | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| `len` | `*len-` | level; along, at this depth |
| `nub` | `*nub-` | down; a working descent |
| `dolnub` | `*dol-nub` | pitward; a deep, committed dive |
| `ū` | `*hau` | up; surfaceward |
| `ūtel` | `*hau-tel` | breach-up; an overshooting ascent |

"Up" being a single exhaled vowel, `ū`, is the language's quiet joke about where its heart is.

**System 04 · `neks` — the gap as courtesy.** The gap particle survives, heavier — **`neks`** (`*nex-`, cluster kept) — but its pragmatics drifted. Where Sūchel `ne` marks what physics *forbids* narrating, Nubhel `neks` marks *any interval your listener did not share*: a returning diver folds years of away-time into one mark and moves on, *out of kindness*. Example: `en yed-t-ó=mi. neks. en len ve-ó=ka.` — "I left. — . I am here, level, in shared time."

**The reconstruction proof.** Nubhel's plain mood is `-ó` where Sūchel has `-á`. D-4 rounds only *stressed* `*a`, so the suffix could only have become `-ó` if it already carried the stress when the daughters split. The sound change is therefore fossil evidence that "the mood carries the beat" was late Old Pelagic prosody, not a Sūchel invention — both fleets inherited the truth-beat from the ancestor.

#### Across the cognate gap — false friends

A crosser and a diver understand most of each other, which is what makes the failures dangerous. Core correspondences, with the traps flagged: `*gel-` jel/yel (the shibboleth row extends `gel · jel · ghel · zel · dzel · yel`); `*kel` chel/hel; `*suw-kel` Sūchel/Sūhel; `*sow-orn` Sōrn/Sōrn (identical); `*kad` kad/kod; `*wer-` ver/ber; `*nub-` nuv/nub; `*ged-` jed/yed; **`*tan-` tan/ton** (trap: Nubhel *ton* sounds like Sūchel *tem* on a bad channel — hold-vs-pattern confusions are a known accident class); **`*mar/*mor` → mor** (merger); **`*wel/*bel` → bel** (merger); `*nex-` ne/neks; `*sa…*dak` sa…dak / so…dok (the **counting test** — customs makes you count to ten, and the vowels file your fleet); `*hep` hep/ep (Sūchel keeps initial h, Nubhel killed all h — divers surface without breath: `hōl` vs `ōl`); `*suw-il/*nub-i` sīl·nuvi / Sūl·Nūbi.

#### Lexicon and texts

The page provides a **30-entry working lexicon** (`Nubhel`, `Nūbi`, `Sūl`, `Sūhel`, `hel`, `yel`, `yed`, `kod`, `kodur` "beacon-cycle, the unit of the offset," `ber`, `bur`, `bos`, `bel`, `mor`, `ton`, `ron`, `ōl`, `ū`, `ūtel`, `len`, `nub`, `dolnub`, `neks`, `tem`, `ān`, `nā` "ship," `nāmai`, `enmai`, `on` 3SG, `so…dok`). Three texts illustrate it: **a diver's homecoming** (the determinate regime in two lines — away-years folded into one `neks`, return re-anchored to the beacon, dilation declared as a number; where a crosser's homecoming ends in an unanswerable question, a diver's ends in an *invoice*); **the parallel sentence** (`enmai nav-mai nuv jed-a=mi` in Sūchel vs `enmai nā-mai nub yed-ó=mi` in Nubhel — four words in five land across the gap, and what trips the ear is exactly the rivalry: `nav/nā, nuv/nub, jed/yed, -a/-ó`); and **the code-switch showpiece** (`on dolnub yed-t-ó=mi, yel-ol —` then the borrowed Sūchel clause `an sū-t-eshe=zu` for the crossing, then back to Nubhel `on ōl-t-ó=nu, +so kodur`). The grammatical hole made visible: Nubhel carries narration to the seam's edge, **must borrow Sūchel for the crossing itself**, then resumes on the far side — the language shift *is* the seam.

## XI · Factions, Powers, and the Knowledge Base

Everything in the QTR archive is presented as the internal literature of a single civilization — the fleets that cross the depths and reaches of the one universe — and issued under the authority of one institution. This chapter catalogues the named powers of that setting, then steps back to describe the archive itself: the fifteen documents that make up the knowledge base, and the computational model on which the whole edifice is quietly built.

### Factions and Powers

The QTR sources are, first and last, a *physics* archive; named political bodies are deliberately few, and most of the "factions" domain of the lexicon is really a domain of *doctrine* — who holds which verdict — rather than of armies. The named powers that do appear are the following.

> **The Pelagian Assembly** — the standing authority of the setting, and the byline of the entire archive. Every page in both subsystems carries `PELAGIAN ASSEMBLY` in its status bar. It is at once the *teaching authority* on Κ-crossing doctrine and the *keeper of the codex/lexicon* itself.

The Assembly's power is doctrinal and epistemic rather than martial. It teaches **the Absolute Verdict** on the `Κ5` controversy — the consensus that nothing survives a crossing of the curvature limit `Κ` (annihilation-into-genesis). Crucially, the codex is candid that this authority is *manufactured by the frontier it describes*: the Absolute Verdict is **unfalsifiable from the safe side** because `Κ` never returns a counterexample, so "the consensus is certain precisely because the limit censors every witness who could contradict it." The Assembly is thus the institution that benefits from `Κ` remaining closed. Its liturgical register (see the languages) is the confident mood — plain `T`, beacon-anchored — and its authority "rests on a verdict that is unfalsifiable from the safe side: a certainty manufactured by the frontier it describes."

- **The fleets** (the crossers, informally *the horizontal fleet*) — the spacefaring civilization at the heart of the world: the ships and crews who ride the vacuum and cross the seam `𝔍`. Their common tongue is **Sūchel, the Crossing-Speech**, "the tongue that binds the fleets." A civilization "that can almost cross its universe entirely does not speak one language — it speaks a family," so the fleets are bound by Sūchel while each settled world keeps its own sister-tongue.

- **The Diver Fleet** (the *vertical fleet*, the *divers*) — the rival fleet, distinguished not by allegiance but by *physics of practice*. Where crossers cross the seam (the `T•` gap), the divers descend the depth axis. In the setting's phrasing, "divers go fast to stay in one universe," "come home old," "built their verbs around the offset, not the gap," and "live in the determinate one" (the determinate timeline of depth-travel, where the twin paradox is inverted and the traveller ages *more*). Their tongue is **Nubhel, the Deep-Speech** — the same proto-language as Sūchel, worn differently, grammaticalizing the *offset* rather than the seam.

- **The mythical ship** — a single obsessive craft, "believed by almost none," that chases the rumoured **Formless mouths** and attempts the **Aperture gambit** to prove **the Madman's Theorem** (the `T⁺` verdict that a ship can hold a `Κ`-fracture open, pass through intact, and return). It is "the only agent pursuing the one event that could break the closed epistemic trap," and therefore the one thing the Assembly needs to be false. The Assembly denies the Formless mouths exist; the mythical ship is built to find them.

- **The Irrationals** — also called the ***Ricercatori del Limite*** and, in-world, **the Wolori** — an order of science "that follows Logos." They are the second custodian of the ancestral language **Old Pelagic**, which they keep *working* (as the living technical register **Lorkel**, "ratio-speech") rather than embalmed. They stand against the Assembly's doctrinal side, **Doxa / the consensus**, which keeps **Liturgical Pelgar** frozen under the creed *«tutto è connesso con tutto»* ("everything is connected to everything"). The two share one mirrored motive: the Assembly keeps Old Pelagic *because it cannot describe the seam* (so no crossing can contradict doctrine), and the Wolori keep it *for the same reason* (so inquiry is protected from doctrine). This "custody battle over a dead language is one of the quietest wars in the setting."

- **The settled worlds** — the daughter-branches of the fleet civilization, each named with its own sister-tongue: **Mars** (`Rudgar`, the conservative tongue of the oldest continuously settled ground; home of Assembly cantors and Wolori lectors), the ocean moon **Maren** (`Sel`, the liquid tongue), and the capital world **Belgar** (`Beltsel`, the eroded prestige tongue).

- **Cosmetic civilization names** — in the model's default scenario, individual civilizations draw names from codex vocabulary purely as colour: the **Casimir League**, the **Penrose Sodality**, the **Seam-Wardens**, and the **Formless Seekers**. The lore file explicitly flags these as cosmetic, not deep canon.

The three `Κ5` verdicts function as the setting's real fault-lines, each mapped to a truth-value of `ΛL` and to a body that holds it: the **Absolute Verdict** (`T`) held by *the Pelagian Assembly, every instrument, and common sense*; the **Tragic Truth** (`T•`) known to *no one who could report it*; and the **Madman's Theorem** (`T⁺`) chased by *a single mythical ship*. Together they form a **closed epistemic trap** — the consensus unfalsifiable, the truth unconfirmable, and the only possible breaker the theory everyone mocks — which is what keeps `Κ` a live and dangerous frontier that "the whole civilization has an interest in keeping closed."

Beneath that literary surface sits a genuine **computational model** — the "deeptime" simulation — of which the codex is the worldbuilding skin. Two linked code artifacts carry it.

**`deeptime` — the standalone engine.** A working implementation of the *Complete Mathematical Model of Deep-Time Civilizational Evolution*. It is a **hybrid stochastic dynamical network** with the structure

> `dX = F dt + Σ dW + J dN`

— ordinary differential equations (`F`, drift), graph diffusion, stochastic differential equations (`Σ dW`), and jump processes (`J dN`) over a state-dependent graph topology. Its universe is *ordinary spacetime coupled to a nonlocal, navigable **Field Ocean***, and its central conceit is that "civilizations are temporary configurations of matter, information, energy and Field structure across deep time." It tracks a 15-variable civilization state, integrates single histories or Monte-Carlo ensembles (estimating probability questions such as `P(K≥3)`), and ships a self-contained web interface (custom SVG charts, no external JS, numpy the only dependency). Every parameter is tagged by epistemic status — **E** (empirical), **X** (extrapolative), **S** (speculative).
