# The QTR System — Field Summary

*Single-Universe, Multi-Depth Canon · Narrative present ≈ 50 Gyr · Issued under the Pelagian Assembly*

> The complete reference is `SYSTEM.md`. This is the printable digest.

---

## 1 · What QTR is

The **Quantum Theory of Relativity** treats spacetime as **emergent**: it is not fundamental but
grows from a partial order of **relational quantum events**. Everything the drama needs happens
inside **one physical universe**. Civilizations sail the structured vacuum — the *Field* — and can
travel between stars, galaxies, cosmological distances, quantum-vacuum regimes, deeper states of the
QTR substrate, topologically shortened routes, and causally-remote regions or charts **of that same
universe**. They **cannot** travel to other universes; other universes are a question for the far
horizon of knowledge, not a destination on any map.

partial order of events · **P2 Amplitude** — every finite sub-history carries a complex amplitude ·
**P3 Frames** — no amplitude is absolute; all are relative to an internal quantum reference frame ·
**P4 Growth** — the order is grown one event at a time ("becoming").

**ΛL truth-values:** `T` classical true · `T⁻` left/IR approach · `T•` seam-truth · `T⁺` right/UV
approach · `F` classical false.

## 3 · The Ontological Cantor Tower (OCT) — state-depths of one universe

The OCT is a transfinite hierarchy of **ontological and informational depths within a single
universe**, ordered by mutual information. Diving deeper shortens the effective Field route, so a
deeper ship is a faster ship. The postulates P1–P4 hold unchanged at every level.

| Level | Meaning | Class | Reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OCT-0** | Effective surface — ordinary emergent spacetime; stars, planets, galaxies, civilizations | Class I | Any point in the home universe |
| **OCT-1** | Deep-vacuum coherence — a deeper coherence regime; very long intra-universal shortcuts | Class II | Remote galactic & intergalactic regions of the same universe |
| **OCT-2** | Cosmological substrate reach — coherence across normally causally-disconnected regions | Class III | Remote causal domains / large-scale structure of the same universe |
| **OCT-ω** | Transfinite internal depth — asymptotic substrate refinement within this universe | Class ω | The asymptotic depth of the one universe |
| **OCT-Ω** | Fixed point of the universe's own self-description | — | Not a place; acknowledged, never crossed |

> **Model note.** The OCT is a *fictional* QTR model of nested resolution within one universe. It is
> not, by itself, evidence for a real multiverse; its levels are state-depths, not separate universes.

## 5 · Navigation — three axes, three doors

- **Depth** — the seam **`𝔍`** / **Idrenes Bridge.** A vertical dive to a deeper OCT rung and back;
  the daily basis of FTL. The seam is the threshold where field language fails — a transition in
  *state-depth*, **not a border between universes**. The un-narratable seam interval is preserved.
- **Adjacency** — the **Penrose bridge.** A one-way, asymmetric throat (black-hole mouth → white-hole
  exit) joining two **remote regions of the same universe**. A round trip needs a second bridge.
- **Constitution** — the curvature limit **`Κ`.** The **internal limit of QTR's own description** —
  the edge past which the theory can no longer say what a region becomes. No ship has crossed `Κ` and
  returned; no instrument confirms it leads to another universe. Approached, never confirmed crossed.

**The `Κ5` verdicts** are three *competing interpretations* of that unreachable boundary — the
**Absolute Verdict** (nothing coherent survives), the **Tragic Truth** (a pattern *might* be
translated beyond the descriptive limit — unconfirmable), and the **Madman's Theorem** (a returning
witness is imagined as the only falsifier; none exists). They ground doctrine and controversy, not a
navigable multiverse.

## 7 · Deep Time — the civilizational ladder

The narrative present sits at **≈ 50 Gyr**: a mature interstellar/intergalactic civilization with deep
Field navigation and large networks of biological, synthetic, and hybrid cognition. Those intergalactic
networks are **precursors — partial organs of a still-forming whole**, not a completed universal mind.

The universe only gradually builds an integrated model of itself:

> individual self → species identity → planetary civilization → galactic network → intergalactic
> network → **universal self-reference (≈ 10¹³ yr)** → **possible trans-universal awareness (≈ 10¹⁴ yr,
> recognition — not travel)**

**Universal self-reference emerges broadly around 10¹³ years; possible trans-universal awareness
approaches only near the late-Stelliferous horizon, around 10¹⁴ years.** These are order-of-magnitude,
model-dependent estimates, not a guaranteed clock — QTR has no universal time, and the Deep-Time axis
is an aggregate cosmological coordinate.

## 8 · Other universes — status, not deletion

The concept is retained but its **status is changed**. Other universes belong to the late horizon of
knowledge and may appear only as an **OPEN QUESTION**, a **MODEL ASSUMPTION**, a **PHILOSOPHICAL
INTERPRETATION**, a **DOCTRINAL CLAIM**, or a **POSSIBLE EXTENSION** — never a ship route, a map
coordinate, an energy tier, or a verified destination. Cosmic fates and Penrose aeons are competing,
stochastic scenarios for *this* universe's lifecycle, not an already-existing multiverse container.

## 9 · The Languages

**Sūchel**, the crossing-speech of the fleets, and its family — **Old Pelagic**, **Navcher**,
**Nubhel** — grammaticalize the physics: five veridical moods (mapping ΛL), temporal anchors, **depth
directionals** (still valid, because the OCT is a real state-depth hierarchy), and the gap particle for
the un-narratable seam interval. They are built by the **Peterson Method** — a *linguistic construction
method only* (speakers, history, sound change, grammaticalization). It is **not** QTR's mathematical or
physical foundation, and not a derivation of the cosmology.

## 10 · Epistemic honesty

Everything down to the seam rests on **real quantum field theory** (Casimir, Unruh, Hawking, Parker,
Penrose conformal structure). The seam is exactly where rigorous physics ends and **invented QTR
physics** begins. Deep-Time equations are model assumptions or simulation machinery; the civilizational
ladder is a narrative and stochastic framework; the "universe becoming conscious" is a
narrative/philosophical hypothesis built from distributed cognition — not established cosmology.
